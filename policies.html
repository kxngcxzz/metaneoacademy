Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 2026

Who we are

Meta Neo is a digital education brand operated by Casey Mungofa, based in the United Kingdom. We provide digital products and educational content relating to social media growth and monetisation. Our website is metaneoacademy.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What data we collect

  • Email address — when you sign up for our free guide or join our email list
  • Name — when you complete a contact or booking form
  • Payment information — processed securely by Whop; Meta Neo does not store card details
  • Usage data — analytics about how visitors use our website, collected only with your consent

We use the following processors: Whop (payments and product delivery), Mailchimp (email list), Google Analytics, Meta and Whop (analytics and advertising, only with consent).

    Legal basis for processing

    • Consent — when you sign up to our email list
    • Contractual necessity — when you purchase a product
    • Consent — for analytics and advertising cookies, which load only if you accept them

    How long we keep your data

    • Email address — until you unsubscribe, or after two years of no opens or clicks, whichever comes first
    • Purchase records — six years, as UK tax law requires
    • Analytics data — retained by Google Analytics for 14 months, and only collected if you accepted cookies

    Where your data goes

    Our processors are Whop (payments and product delivery), Mailchimp (email list), and, only with your consent, Google, Meta and Whop for analytics and advertising. Some of these transfer data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on their own approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or adequacy regulations.

    Your rights under UK GDPR

    • Access the personal data we hold about you
    • Request correction of inaccurate data
    • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
    • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. unsubscribe from emails)
    • Object to processing, or ask us to restrict it
    • Request a portable copy of the data you gave us
    • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

    To exercise any of your data rights, email hello@metaneoacademy.com or message @_metaneo_ on Instagram. We will respond within 30 days as required by UK GDPR.

    Cookie Policy

    Effective date: November 2026

    Nothing is set until you accept

    When you first arrive you are asked to accept or reject non-essential cookies. Rejecting takes one click, exactly like accepting. If you reject, or if you simply ignore the choice, no analytics or advertising script is loaded at all.

    What loads if you accept

    • Google Analytics 4 — how many people visit, which pages they read, and how they found us. Used to improve the site.
    • Meta Pixel — measures whether our Instagram and Facebook advertising works, and allows us to show ads to people who have visited.
    • Whop Pixel — connects a visit to this site with a purchase on Whop, so we can tell which pages lead to sales.

    These set cookies in your browser and share data with Google, Meta and Whop respectively, who act as our processors. They may transfer data outside the UK under their own safeguards.

    Strictly necessary storage

    Two things are stored on your device regardless, because the site cannot work correctly without them, and neither tracks you: your cookie choice itself, so you are not asked again on every page; and a record that an order confirmation has already been counted, so refreshing that page does not report a second sale.

    Changing your mind

    Use the Cookies link in the footer of any page to reopen the choice. You can also clear cookies and site data from your browser settings at any time.

    We rely on your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and the UK GDPR. You may withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect anything you have already purchased.
